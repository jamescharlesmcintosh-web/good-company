The film  /  What it's about

Two crews.
One bad idea.
Then a good one.

MAYA is building a product nobody asked for out of a kitchen she doesn't technically rent. DANI is running a supper club from a van that isn't hers either. They've each got their people, their jokes, their whole world — and absolutely no idea the other one exists.

GOOD COMPANY follows the two crews across one long summer of near-misses, shared venues and one very bad pitch night. When they finally collide, they get a friendship, a business, and an adventure none of them planned.

“It's a film about the moment you stop building something alone and start building it with people.”

Ines Marchetti  /  Director
ACT 01

Separate worlds

Two crews, two ambitions, two very different definitions of \u201Cmaking it work.\u201D We meet everyone at full speed.

ACT 02

The near-misses

The same venue. The same week. The same idea, almost. They keep being one degree apart until a pitch night goes very wrong for both of them.

ACT 03

Good company

They merge. It does not go smoothly. Then it does. The last third is warmer, funnier and busier than anything either crew managed alone.

        Four steps.
        No mystery.

        No fee to apply, ever. We read every submission. If we'd like to see more you'll hear from us within 10 working days.
