Terms  /  Rate & contract

What you get.

Every role is paid. There is no fee to apply, no fee to audition, and no fee at any other point. If anyone asks you for money, it is not us.

Daily rate £150 per day BECTU-aligned feature rate for all roles, lead and supporting alike. Paid within 14 days of wrap.
Contract Equity / BECTU terms Standard artist contract. Non-union performers are engaged on the same terms — you don't need to be a member.
Expenses Travel & accommodation Standard class travel to and from the shoot base, plus accommodation for anyone outside a daily commute.
On set Feeds, hours, care Breakfast and lunch every day. 12-hour working day including breaks. A named point of contact for anything at all.

Schedule  /  The casting timeline

When things happen.

Submissions are read on a rolling basis as they arrive. You don't need to wait for the deadline — but we do stop reading after it.

Now – 30 Apr

Submissions open

Applications are read as they come in. Early applications get early replies — if we like what we see we'll ask for a self-tape within days, not weeks.

Late Apr

Self-tapes

We send sides and a brief to everyone we'd like to see more from. Two minutes, one take, plain background. Send by 8 May.

Mid – late May

Chemistry reads

Group reads in London and Manchester, with an online slot for anyone who can't travel. You'll read with the people you'd be playing opposite.

From 15 May

Offers, rolling

No waiting until everyone's been seen. As soon as we know, you know. Contracts and schedules confirmed before the end of June.

14 Jul – 22 Aug

Principal photography

Six weeks, split between London and Manchester. Weekday shoots, some weekend exteriors. Full schedule issued four weeks ahead.

2027

Release

Festival premiere targeted for spring, theatrical in autumn. Everyone in the film is credited and invited to the premiere.

Access  /  Travel & care

We'll get you there.

Nobody should lose a role because of a train fare or a step in a doorway. Tell us what you need in the application — access requirements, caring responsibilities, anything at all — and we'll plan around it rather than the other way round.

01

Travel

Standard class return to the shoot base, booked and paid by us. Not reimbursed later — booked in advance.

02

Accommodation

Provided for anyone outside a daily commute. En-suite where available, always your own room.

03

Access

Step-free sets, quiet rooms, flexible call times, BSL interpreters on request. Ask and we'll sort it.

04

Under-18s

Chaperoned and licensed, with reduced hours. Applications welcome with a guardian listed.

Questions  /  The honest answers

FAQ.

If it isn't here, ask us directly. We would rather answer twice than have you guess.

Do I need an agent or previous credits?
No. Most of this cast will be people we haven't worked with before. If you have credits, send them. If you don't, send nothing — tell us in a paragraph what you'd bring instead.
Is there any fee at any stage?
No. Not to apply, not to audition, not for a self-tape, not for headshots, not for training. We pay you — that is the only money that should ever move. If anyone contacts you claiming to be from this production and asks for payment, it isn't us.
Can I apply for more than one role?
Yes. Apply once and tell us which roles you'd suit in the note — or pick “General / open to any role”. We would rather see one good application than four rushed ones.
What does the self-tape need to be?
We send sides when we want one — don't tape anything before then. When we do: two minutes, one take, plain background, natural light, sound over picture. No editing, no music, no title cards.
I'm not based in London. Should I still apply?
Yes. Half the shoot is in Manchester and we travel people in. Tell us where you are in the application so we can plan from the start.
Are the crews gender or ethnicity specific?
Only where the breakdown says so. Otherwise every role is open to anyone. Non-binary, trans and disabled performers are actively encouraged to apply for any role in the breakdown.
How and when do I hear back?
Everyone hears back. If we'd like to see more, within 10 working days. If it's a no, you'll hear that too — we don't leave people wondering.
What happens to my details?
Held for this production only, never shared or sold, and deleted 12 months after wrap. Ask and we'll delete them sooner.
